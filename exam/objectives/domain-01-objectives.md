# Domain 1 Objectives — Architecture

**Weight: 31%**

1. Understand Cloud Services, Compute, and Storage.
2. Understand micro-partitions and pruning.
3. Select warehouse size for a workload.
4. Distinguish Scale Up and Scale Out.
5. Understand multi-cluster modes and policies.
6. Distinguish the three caches.
7. Recognize Standard Gen 1, Standard Gen 2, and Snowpark-optimized warehouses.
8. Recognize Snowflake interfaces and drivers/connectors.
9. Understand the object hierarchy.
10. Understand table and view types.
11. Understand parameter precedence.
12. Compare Snowflake editions.
13. Recognize Notebooks, Streamlit, Snowpark, Cortex, and Snowflake ML use cases.

### Must-know
- Result cache: 24 h, Cloud Services, global, no warehouse required.
- Warehouse cache is lost on suspension/resizing.
- Billing: per second, 60-second minimum per start.
- Micro-partitions: approximately 50–500 MB with min/max metadata.
